idfon voices — blend vs sources

Nine candidate voices for idfon, each a weighted blend of two Phonon catalog voices (their conditioning latents averaged — local math, no API). Every line plays the same text three ways: source A, the blend, source B. Pick what sounds familiar without being anyone in particular. Run scripts/phonon-voice-compare.py to re-render.
